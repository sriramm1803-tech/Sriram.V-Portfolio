Project

Coffee Sales Dashboard

Power BISQLAdvanced Excel
115.43KTotal sales
3,636Transactions
31.75Average price
  • Built a single-page Power BI dashboard on coffee shop transactions covering revenue, order volume, product mix and time-of-day sales.
  • Cleaned the data with SQL and Excel (Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, conditional formatting) before loading it into Power BI.
  • Designed KPI cards that show total revenue and the top-selling product at a glance.
  • Combined revenue and order count in a monthly trend chart to track performance and seasonality.
  • Found that Americano is the best seller, followed by Latte and Cappuccino, and that sales are split almost evenly across the day: night 33.82%, afternoon 33.8%, morning 32.38%.
  • Spotted two sales peaks in the year, in March and October, with orders rising and falling alongside revenue.
